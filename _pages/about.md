Pham-Minh-Tri Hoang received the B.Eng. degree in Advanced Program Electrical and Electronics Engineering from [Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology](https://hcmut.edu.vn/), Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. He is currently pursuing a Master's degree at [Memorial University of Newfoundland](https://www.mun.ca/), Canada, where he holds a scholarship under the supervision of Prof. Trung Q. Duong, Canada Excellence Research Chair in Next Generation Communication Technologies.

His research interests include **wireless communication systems**, **machine learning**, and **quantum computing**.

Beyond research, he works with machine learning, data analysis, and time series data, mostly in Python. Before graduate school, he completed internships in analog and mixed-signal IC design at Viettel Semiconductor Technology Division and National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan.
